Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining costs nothing, and so does every book on the site that is free to read. For books still in copyright we show you a free preview, a library copy or where to buy it.

Who can join the community?

Students at school, college or university, and apprentices, aged 13 or over. If you are under 13, ask a parent or teacher to get in touch with us.

Do I need to join to read the books?

No, every page is open. Joining puts you on the student list, so you hear first about new reading lists, study tools and OMT training.

Is it safe to use at school?

OMT only links to official sources, publishers, open editions and libraries. There are no ads, and the AI study buddy is built for learning, not shortcuts.

Can my school use OMT with a class?

Yes. Teachers can share any shelf or reading list with a link, and we are happy to put together lists for your courses. Use the contact form to tell us what you teach.
