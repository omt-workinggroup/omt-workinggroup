Quick checklist before you present

One idea per slide

Write a headline that states the point of each slide.

Readable from the back

Body text at 24 pt or larger, with strong contrast.

Show, don't list

Use a clear chart or image instead of a wall of bullets.

Rehearse twice

Time yourself out loud and know your opening line.
