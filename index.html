Who we are

A multidisciplinary partner for the assets that matter.

  • Technical expertise
  • Field experience
  • Quality & safety first

OMT – Working Group is an integrated operations, maintenance, and training company providing specialized services across renewable energy, power systems, civil works, information and communication technology (ICT), and telecommunications.

The company is structured to deliver reliable, efficient, and sustainable solutions through a combination of technical expertise, field experience, and a strong commitment to quality and safety. Our core mandate is to ensure the smooth operation and long-term performance of critical infrastructure assets while supporting capacity development through targeted training programs.

OMT – Working Group operates with a results-oriented approach, guided by international best practices and industry standards. We partner with public and private sector clients to deliver turnkey solutions that enhance operational efficiency, minimize downtime, and extend the lifecycle of assets.

Our multidisciplinary capability, coupled with a dedicated team of professionals, positions OMT as a trusted partner in the delivery of innovative, dependable, and cost-effective operations, maintenance, and training services.
