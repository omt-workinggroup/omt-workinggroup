Staff picks

Four books we keep recommending

All books

Computer Networks: A Systems Approach

Peterson & Davie

The one we hand to people starting on a transport or IP team. Read the first three chapters properly, then dip in as needed.

Free online

Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces

Remzi & Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau

Short chapters, a sense of humour, and the explanation of virtual memory that finally makes it click.

Free online

Site Reliability Engineering

Beyer, Jones, Petoff & Murphy

How Google runs production. If you only read one chapter before your first on-call week, make it the one on monitoring.

Free online

Private 5G: A Systems Approach

Peterson, Sunay & Davie

The clearest tour of the 5G core and RAN we've found that isn't a 3GPP specification.

Free online

Why this exists

Most of us learned from borrowed PDFs and dead links.

OMT is the shelf we wished we'd had: the good books, sorted by the work you actually do, each one linked back to the people who wrote it.

We don't publish anything and we don't host copies. If a book is free, we send you to the free edition. If it isn't, we say so and tell you where to borrow or buy it.
