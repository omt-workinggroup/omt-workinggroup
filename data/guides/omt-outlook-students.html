Writing a professional email

  • Subject line — specific: "ECO 201 – Question about Assignment 2 deadline".
  • Greeting — use the person's title: "Dear Dr Okeke,".
  • Opening — say who you are if they may not know: "I'm a second-year student in your Tuesday class."
  • Request — be brief and clear about what you need.
  • Close — "Kind regards," followed by your full name and student ID.
  • Write from your university account, proofread, and attach files as PDFs with clear names.

Example

Subject: ECO 201 – Request for feedback on Essay 1

Dear Dr Okeke,

I'm a student in your ECO 201 Tuesday class. I received 58% on
Essay 1 and would like to understand how to improve my analysis.
Would you be available during office hours this week?

Kind regards,
Amina Yusuf
Student ID: 2023-0451

Organise your inbox

  • Create a folder per course (right-click your inbox › New Folder).
  • Use Rules to move messages from a course mailing list into its folder automatically.
  • Flag emails you need to act on, and clear the flags once you're done.

Plan your week with Calendar

  • Add your timetable as recurring events (New Event › Make Recurring).
  • Block study sessions the same way you would add a class.
  • Add assignment deadlines with a reminder two or three days before.
