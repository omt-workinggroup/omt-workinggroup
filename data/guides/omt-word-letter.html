Structure of a formal letter

  1. Your address and contact details — top of the page.
  2. Date — written in full, e.g. 15 October 2026.
  3. Recipient's name, title and address.
  4. Subject line — one line that says what the letter is about.
  5. Greeting — "Dear Dr Adeyemi," or "Dear Sir or Madam," if you don't know the name.
  6. Opening paragraph — why you are writing.
  7. Body — the details, one topic per paragraph.
  8. Closing paragraph — what you would like to happen next.
  9. Sign-off — "Yours sincerely" when you used their name, "Yours faithfully" with "Dear Sir or Madam" (British convention); then your name.

Tips

  • Keep it to one page and use a plain, readable font.
  • Be polite and direct; state your request clearly.
  • Proofread names and titles carefully.
