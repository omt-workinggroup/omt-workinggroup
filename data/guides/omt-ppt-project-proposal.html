The 9-slide flow

  1. Title — project name and team.
  2. The problem — who is affected and why it matters.
  3. Proposed solution — what you will build or do.
  4. Objectives — three to five specific, measurable goals.
  5. Scope — what is included and what is not.
  6. Timeline — phases and milestones.
  7. Budget and resources — costs, equipment, people.
  8. Risks and mitigation — what could go wrong and your plan.
  9. The ask — the approval, funding or support you need.

Write objectives that convince

Avoid "Improve the library system." Write "Reduce the time to find a book in the library catalogue from 5 minutes to under 1 minute by June." Panels look for objectives that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound (SMART).

Tips

  • Lead with the problem, not the technology.
  • Show the timeline as a simple table or Gantt-style bar chart.
  • End with a clear request so the audience knows what decision you need.
