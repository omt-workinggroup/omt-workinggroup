1. Use styles for headings

Select a heading and choose Home › Styles › Heading 1 (use Heading 2 for subsections). Styles keep your formatting consistent and make the table of contents and navigation pane work.

2. Set the body text

Most assignments ask for a 12 pt font and 1.5 or double line spacing. Right-click the Normal style › Modify to set the font and spacing once for the whole document. Always follow your lecturer's instructions if they differ.

3. Add an automatic table of contents

Put the cursor where the contents should go, then References › Table of Contents. After editing, click the table and choose Update Table.

4. Page numbers and a title page

Insert › Page Number. To hide the number on the title page, double-click the header or footer and tick Different First Page.

5. Citations and references

Under References › Citations & Bibliography, choose your style (for example APA or Harvard), use Insert Citation as you write, then Bibliography at the end. Check the result against your institution's style guide; automatic tools sometimes format details incorrectly.

6. Before you submit

  • Open View › Navigation Pane to check your structure.
  • Run Review › Word Count.
  • Export to PDF if your submission system accepts it.
