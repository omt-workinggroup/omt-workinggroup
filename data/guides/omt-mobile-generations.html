The core network, generation by generation

  • 2G/3G: the circuit-switched core handles voice (MSC), and the packet core (SGSN and GGSN) handles data. HLR stores subscriber data.
  • 4G EPC (Evolved Packet Core): the MME handles signalling and mobility, the SGW and PGW carry user traffic and connect to the internet, and the HSS stores subscriber data.
  • 5G Core: a service-based architecture of network functions. The AMF handles access and mobility, the SMF manages sessions, the UPF forwards user traffic, and the UDM stores subscriber data.

5G terms you'll hear

  • NSA vs SA: Non-Standalone 5G uses an LTE anchor and the 4G core. Standalone 5G uses the 5G Core end to end.
  • FR1 and FR2: FR1 is the sub-6 GHz bands (wide coverage). FR2 is millimetre-wave (very high capacity, short range).
  • gNB: the 5G base station (the 4G one is the eNB). It can be split into a central unit (CU) and distributed unit (DU), as in Open RAN designs.
  • Network slicing: running several logical networks, each with its own performance, on shared infrastructure.
