What's inside

  • Assignments — course, task, due date, status and days left (calculated with TODAY()).
  • Grades — each assessment's score and weight. The weighted average per course uses SUMPRODUCT.

How the weighted average works

If a test is worth 30% and an exam 70%, your result is score1 × 0.3 + score2 × 0.7. The template uses:

=SUMPRODUCT(scores, weights) / SUM(weights)

Dividing by the total weight means the result stays correct even before every assessment has been graded.

How to use it

  1. List each assignment with its due date. The Days left column counts down automatically.
  2. Change Status to "Done" when you submit.
  3. Enter scores (out of 100) and weights (as percentages) on the Grades sheet as results come in.
