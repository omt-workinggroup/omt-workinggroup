The 10-slide structure

  1. Title — topic, your name, course, date.
  2. Outline — three to five sections.
  3. Background — what the audience needs to know first.
  4. Problem or research question — one clear sentence.
  5. Method — how you investigated it.
  6. Findings (1) — one key result, ideally as a chart.
  7. Findings (2) — the next key result.
  8. Discussion — what the results mean and their limits.
  9. Conclusion — the three points to remember.
  10. References and questions.

Using the template

  • Every slide has placeholder text and speaker notes explaining what to put there.
  • Change the colours for the whole deck in Design › Variants › Colors.
  • Delete or duplicate slides from the thumbnail panel; your formatting is preserved.

Timing

As a rough guide, plan about one to two minutes per content slide and rehearse out loud at least twice with a timer.
